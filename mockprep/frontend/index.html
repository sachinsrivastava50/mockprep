Master Job Interviews with Objective AI Feedback

MockPrep is a full-stack web application that simulates real job interviews. Select a role, answer tailored technical or behavioral questions, and receive an instant weighted score out of 10 with actionable improvement tips and model benchmark answers.

Role-Specific AI Questions

Generates dynamic, real-world questions across Software Engineering, Data Analysis, and Custom roles with Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.

Weighted Scoring (Out of 10)

Multi-dimensional evaluation combining correctness (2.5), clarity (2.0), depth (2.0), relevance (2.0), and communication (1.5).

Database History & Analytics

Every session and question result is persisted in PostgreSQL / SQLite with interactive Chart.js score trend tracking over time.
